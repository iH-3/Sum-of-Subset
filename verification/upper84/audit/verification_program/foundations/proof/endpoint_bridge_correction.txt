Defect in the prose
The proof calls [h+1,L-h] filled. If H is nonempty and h=max H, L-h is
itself one of the omitted upper holes. The filled central interval is
[h+1,L-h-1]. The x-shift and the stated final central interval require the
same minus-one correction. "Overlap" must allow adjacency of integer intervals.

Exact retained statement and corrected proof
Let B be the existing prefix of m DISTINCT positive integers, L=sum B, and
S=FS(B)=[0,L]_Z minus(H union(L-H)), where H is its lower endpoint-hole set,
q=|H|, h=max H (h=q=0 if H is empty), and 2h<L. Let the next coefficient
x>max B be integral and have excess increment e_x<=E:
 |S union(x+S)|-|S|=m+1+e_x.
Retain the original hypothesis
 m>=max(h,1+floor((1+sqrt(9+8(E+2q+2h)))/2)).

Distinct positive integrality gives L>=T_m and x>max B>=m>=h. The strict
threshold gives T_m-2h-2q>m+1+E. If x>=L-2h, x+S has at least
 min(x,L+1)-2q >= L-2h-2q >= T_m-2h-2q
points above L. That contradicts the permitted increment. Therefore
x<L-2h, equivalently x<=L-2h-1 by integrality.

Both [h+1,L-h-1]_Z and [x+h+1,x+L-h-1]_Z are filled. The second left
endpoint is at most L-h, one above the first right endpoint. The intervals
therefore overlap or abut and fill [h+1,L+x-h-1]_Z.

Since x>h, adjoining x cannot fill a lower hole in H and cannot create a
new lower hole. Subset complementation in B union{x} makes the upper holes
exactly L+x-H. The central interval is filled, so there are no other holes:
 S union(x+S)=[0,L+x]_Z minus(H union(L+x-H)).
This also covers H empty, with both endpoints supplied by subset sums.
Thus the new support has cardinality L+x+1-2q versus L+1-2q before,
and its increment is x=m+1+e_x, as asserted.
