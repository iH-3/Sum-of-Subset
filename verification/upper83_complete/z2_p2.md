## Restore leading activity in Z2

If leading v is empty, delta>=K and the source inequality
K-delta>=I+tau-8 gives I+tau<=8, 31<=r_v<=36 and
u=delta-K<=8-I-tau. Both leadings cannot be empty because K>=151>u.
The opposite leading has an integral shift. If at least two of the
opposite four own centres are holes, all four own centres on v are
retained and determine integral v-coordinates. Otherwise three
opposite own centres together with its integral leading shift determine
all opposite coordinates. Then an active C6_v or C7_v gives integral
lambda_v; if both are holes all four own centres on v are retained.
Thus lambda_v is integral in all cases. The usual two-witness residue
argument, now with both leading shifts integral, gives all outer
coordinates integral; it does not need a hit in both leadings.

Every other retained singleton has native loss<=u<=8, so is normal:
a BAD centre has at most max(r_s,r_t)+4<=40 hits and loss>=36.
Since A>=K>=151, e_v<=318. Thus
S_v-2M_v>=T31+2*31+6-318=246>0. A leading centre in
[1-2M_v,S_v] would hit via0 or2M_v. If lambda_v>S_v, at least
two of its four own centres remain retained and HIGH, each losing
r_v+5>u points. Hence lambda_v<=-2M_v.

All four cross centres on v are then LOW. In A this removes three
cross singletons, impossible. In B the two exact holes must be
C6_v,C7_v; both paired resources have only the opposite contributor.
All own centres are retained. Normal V_v has
V_v>=72-r_v-u. But V_v=lambda_v+v4<=X_v-M_v, so
X_v>=M_v+72-r_v-u>=76-u>=68. Active C7_o gives
t_o>=2X_o+X_v+g_o+1>69, contradicting active O_o's cap12.
Thus both Z2 leadings are active. The same residue argument proves
outer integrality without any exceptional leading cases remaining.

interval, their mass excess over T_n is at least n-1. Indeed, if
the first greedy failure follows k entries, all n-k subsequent

## No two caps

The common four-top and star calculation gives
4I+4tau+delta<=82+6w if both cap predicates hold.
For P2 this contradicts tau>=61+2z,w<=z directly.
For Z2 it gives tau+delta<=82. If any C7_v is active, the valid

## Four exact beta/O exclusions

Suppose beta_v,O_v are active. The sharp double cap says
2t_v<=17+w/2; the opposite O is the first hole. Let W be the
other whole hole. All listed native resources below are original,
distinct retained resources; every leading is kept at its full K
capacity. Roots explicitly retain their native loss in the forest.

1. W is none of V_v,C7_v,C7_o. Choose a full physical-v two-edge
   arm and the first edge of the other, rooted at leading v. The
   unused C7_v has upper image t_v-2X_v-X_o-g_v<=t_v-4, hence
   native loss>=80-t_v. This quantity is <=76 whenever C7_v is
   active; otherwise the assumed case is already impossible.
   The rooted forest gives
   t_v>=K-M+3F-delta+80-t_v.
   Since tau+3F>=198, this implies
   2t_v>=43-w+I+xi. It contradicts the double cap because
   26+I+xi<=3w/2<=21 would be necessary.

   In Z2 this is>=14>8.5. In P2 it is>=46-w>=32>12 by the

## Exhaustive remaining holes and a common terminal

If both O's are holes, all other whole units are retained; B would
give both beta/H caps, so the branch is A and beta has a sole owner.
If only O_v is missing, beta_o cannot contribute because O_o is
active. In B its singleton is the second hole, leaving beta_v,H_v
active and two caps. In A, either the entire beta pair is the second
hole or its sole contributor is v and H_v must be the second hole.
This exhausts exactly {O_v,Bpair},{O_s,O_t},{O_v,H_v} in A.
All alpha,C5,U,V,C7 singletons are retained. Choose the cap side u
opposite the single missing O, or the sole beta owner if both O's
are missing. Actual alpha_o,C5_u activity gives
t_u>=2X+G+2, hence2X+G<=10+w/2.

2X+G>=6F-delta. The universal full-rank kernel tau+6F>=378 then
forces A>=368+I-w/2. Comparing A<=304+w yields

    64+I<=3w/2<=21,

a contradiction. At w0 the margin is64. This closes Z2 and P2
under their common-source hypotheses, without a first-gap table.
