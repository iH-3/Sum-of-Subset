\begin{abstract}
We strengthen the necessary covering model for a hypothetical 89-element
set by counting the overlap between a leading $2D$ component and an own
$D$ translate. Four new inequalities exclude the previously published
exact relaxation witness, each by exactly 72.
We extract this mechanism from the archived size-89 proof, reprove its
actual-set hypotheses and loss allocation, and generate new certificates.
A whole-side deficit also retains target cardinality without double
counting overlapping subgroups. Final verification statistics and the
endpoint conclusion are in Section~\ref{verification}.
This continues the independently audited chain ending at 89; the
stronger archived claim 84 remains a separate audit task.
\end{abstract}

\section{Endpoint reduction and structural domain}
For a finite real set $A$, $\FS(A)$ includes the empty sum and $4A$
allows repeated summands. Let $N$ be the largest possible size with
$\FS(A)\subseteq4A$. The previous independently checked chain proves
$71\le N\le89$ \cite{bound89,audit}. It suffices to exclude size 89.
Such a set contains zero: otherwise adjoining zero preserves feasibility
and gives a forbidden 90-element set. Integralization preserves the zero
coordinate, distinctness, order and the chosen subset-sum identities.
No arbitrary deletion is asserted to preserve feasibility.

The preceding progress report \cite{progress} proves the structural
reduction for this endpoint, independently of its incomplete covering
search. Its nested sets satisfy
\[
 |D|=81,\quad |E|=73,\quad E=P\cup\{0\}\cup(-Q),\quad
 |P|=a,\quad |Q|=b,\quad a+b=72.
\]
On the common primitive core lattice, $D$ is integral. With
\[
 I=(a-36)^2,\quad\Lambda=1333+I,\quad
 \Omega=546-I,\quad C=-883-I,
\]
the inherited exact budgets are
\begin{align}
 \rho_P+\rho_Q+2\vartheta+c+\T&=546-I,\label{budget}\\
 \T&\ge\kappa+152-3z,\qquad
 \T\ge\max\{51,z,152-2z\},\label{sharp}\\
 c&=2|2D|+18\cdot81+85-|\FS(D)|.\label{cdef}
\end{align}
Here $\kappa=|\FS(P)-\FS(Q)|-|\FS(P)|-|\FS(Q)|+1$,
$z=h_P+h_Q-\kappa\ge0$, $h_P,h_Q$ are side-support hull holes,
and all slack terms are nonnegative.

The hypotheses of the inherited structural proof remain intact.
Intrinsic primitive normalization repairs sixteen insufficient inverse
directions. Early prefix retention proves the remaining inner lattice
comparison before \eqref{sharp} is used. The small-side boundary uses
the universal negative prefix floor, not the size-17 non-AP theorem.
Progression and half-step cases are separately handled; surviving
progression cases have $z=0$. Two retained prefixes give the positive-hole
mass and span rows. In the sharpened R budget, defect and peeling
estimates retain the same hole parameter. The low-hole domain is

High-hole states retain the coupled $z$ variable and all three caps
in \eqref{sharp}. No separately maximized caps and holes are combined.

Write $M_p=\max D$, $M_q=-\min D$, $R_p=\max P$, $R_q=\max Q$.
The four outer positive coordinates satisfy
$p_1>p_2>p_3>p_4>M_p$, and similarly on the negative side.
The inherited integer-row-lattice test is retained, including the thirty

\section{The missing raw overlap}
On the positive covering side put
\[
 c_p=p_1-p_2-p_3-p_4,\qquad
 U_0=c_p+2D,\qquad U_i=c_p+p_i+D.
\]
The first is the leading component, the second a retained own component;
both are considered before intersection with the actual target $F_p$.
Put $K=|2D|$ and $d=81$.

\begin{lemma}[Leading-core intersection]\label{intersection}
If $p_i-M_p$ is integral, then
\[
 |U_0\cap U_i|\ge H_i:=2|E|-R_p-R_q-p_i+M_p-1.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The leading component contains $c_p+M_p+E$, since $M_p\in D$ and
$E\subseteq D$. The own component contains $c_p+p_i+E$.
After translating by $c_p+M_p$, these are two integral sets of size $|E|$
inside an integer interval with $R_p+R_q+p_i-M_p+1$ points.
Inclusion-exclusion proves the bound, including when $H_i<0$.
Neither leading contribution nor target fullness is required.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[One outside loss at a time]\label{outside}
Let $L_0=|U_0\setminus F_p|$ and $L_i=|U_i\setminus F_p|$. Then
\[
 K+d-|(U_0\cup U_i)\cap F_p|\ge H_i+\max\{L_0,L_i\}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The left side equals
\[
 |U_0\cap U_i|+|(U_0\cup U_i)\setminus F_p|.
\]
The first term is at least $H_i$ and the second dominates either
individual outside loss. Adding both outside losses is invalid:
for $U_0=U_i=\{0,1\}$ and $F_p=\{1\}$ the deficit is 3,
whereas $|U_0\cap U_i|+L_0+L_i=4$.
\end{proof}
Reflection replaces $D$ by $-D$ and interchanges $p,q$.
This is raw overlap; no extra outside loss is added to a clipped bound.

\section{Rows and simultaneous loss allocation}
The old variable $G_p$, named \texttt{full\_p}, has an actual realization.
Group the leading component with the retained own slots $p0,p1,p2,p3$
and cross slots $p6,p7$; let $J_p$ denote these ordinary labels. Set
\[
 G_p=K+d|J_p|-
 \left|\left(U_0\cup\bigcup_{v\in J_p}U_v\right)\cap F_p\right|.
\]
For an ordinary label $v$, $\lambda_v=l_v+u_v$ counts lower and upper
hull-tail losses, hence does not exceed the actual loss outside $F_p$,
also when $F_p$ has holes. The model's $L_p$ (\texttt{lead\_p}) does not
exceed $L_0$. Bound the larger union by one pair union and the remaining
individual members. For every retained own slot $v=p(i-1)$ this gives
\begin{align}
 G_p&\ge H_i+L_p+\sum_{w\in J_p\setminus\{v\}}\lambda_w,\label{rowlead}\\
 G_p&\ge H_i+\lambda_v+\sum_{w\in J_p\setminus\{v\}}\lambda_w.\label{rowown}
\end{align}
These are alternative lower bounds for one deficit, not independent
savings to be summed. The $q$ group has disjoint labelled components,
so both sign groups can constrain the global budget simultaneously.

The implementation copies the old row
$-G_p+L_p+\sum_{J_p}\lambda_v\le0$.
For \eqref{rowlead} it deletes the selected own loss; for \eqref{rowown}
it deletes $L_p$. It then adds $-R_p-R_q-p_i+M_p$ and uses right side
$1-2|E|=-145$. Slot $p(i-1)$ is mapped to outer coordinate $p_i$.
The negative side is identical. The rows have no $\delta$ coefficient
and recharge no fixed-label losses. Their new coefficients and right
sides are independent of $a,z,S_p,S_q$; the previous positive-hole mass
transport and high-state grouping preserve them unchanged.

\subsection{Whole-side capacity in the two special modes}
In the Z4 modes with both paired units and both largest own slots absent,
each side has seven retained, unpaired ordinary members $V_s$.
Define
\[
 W_s=K+7d-
 \left|\left(U_{0,s}\cup\bigcup_{v\in V_s}U_v\right)\cap F_s\right|,
 \qquad s=p,q.
\]
Because $F_s\subseteq[1,S_s]_{\Z}$ and union cardinality is subadditive,
\begin{align}
 W_s&\ge K+7d-S_s,\label{wholecap}\\
 W_s&\ge L_s+\sum_{v\in V_s}\lambda_v,\\
 W_s&\ge G_s+\sum_{v\in V_s\setminus J_s}\lambda_v.\label{wholefull}
\end{align}
One actual $W_s$ satisfies all three rows. The first uses target
cardinality; the others partition the labelled components into disjoint
families. The last row charges the $G_s$ subgroup only once.

Put $\delta=c-81j$. The inherited 110-score construction gives eight
mandatory high-label losses and a disjoint extra fixed loss $F_{\rm extra}$.
The full covering budget is
\[
 W_p+W_q+8+F_{\rm extra}\le\delta.
\]
It uses each whole-side group once; $G_p,G_q$, their subgroups and the
same ordinary losses are not separately added. The old budgets remain
valid alternatives under this simultaneous actual realization.
The fixed-score catalogue, all integer threshold alternatives, the 26
disjoint nonpositive deletion groups, and exclusions of separately
charged high labels are unchanged.

\section{Removal of the former obstruction}
The exact rational point in
\texttt{data/upper88\_relaxation\_witness.json} remains a valid witness for
the preceding relaxation, not for an actual feasible set.
It has
\[
 R_p=R_q=36,\quad p_4=122,\quad M_p=121,\quad
 q_4=124,\quad M_q=123,\quad G_p=G_q=1.
\]
For slot $p3$,
\[
 H_4=146-36-36-122+121-1=72.
\]
The other losses in its full group sum to 1, and the selected own and
leading losses are zero. Each of \eqref{rowlead} and \eqref{rowown}
therefore requires $G_p\ge73$, contradicting $G_p=1$ by 72.
The negative side gives two further violations of 72.
This exact calculation explains the removed obstruction without
appealing to a numerical infeasibility status.

All parameters $28\le a\le44$ of this formerly obstructed A-mode close.
Only $a=34,35,36,37,38$ need binary trees in the discovered certificate;
the others admit direct integer Farkas certificates. This local result
is separate from the complete root-universe check below.

\section{Exact verification and conclusion}\label{verification}
The verifier reconstructs the inherited root universe, augments only
eligible nested models, and checks both children of each proved binary
disjunction. Coarse rows and the disjunction catalogue are unchanged.
For nonnegative model variables and $Ax\le b$, an accepted integer
certificate satisfies
\[
 y\ge0,\qquad y^TA\ge0,\qquad y^Tb<0.
\]
Parameter intervals use exact quadratic polynomials, checked at endpoints
and the integers adjacent to any relevant interior vertex. No polynomial
is inferred from floating-point samples. Exhaustive mode regenerates
each integer matrix and compares every row with its symbolic version.

Scalar, lattice and hole calculations come from the endpoint-specific
progress proof, not from an archived endpoint conclusion.
The new geometric implications are proved above.
The optimizer only proposes multipliers; failed rational reconstructions
are rejected. The final verifier uses the standard library and exact
integer/rational arithmetic. Actual-set geometry supplements the proofs:
240 finite sets, 2,880 emitted leading-core rows, 1,680 whole-side rows
and 240 allocation checks pass. A negative control rejects the
double-outside-loss shortcut.

\paragraph{Provenance and scope.}
The leading-core and whole-side ideas occur in the archived size-89
modules \texttt{translated\_core\_overlap89.py} and

claim to improve the archived number 84 or determine the exact maximum.
